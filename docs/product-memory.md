# Money product memory

This file records durable product decisions for future work.

- No is an all-in-one bank-information site. Give the fee and its trigger in the page. Put evidence and secondary terms behind disclosures. Readers make their own decisions.
- Start with a quiet search. Suggest at most five banks while typing and show the three product links directly below search. Readers can start from a task without already choosing a bank.
- The wordmark is No, without a period. Use the approved brand blue `#3F31D9` for the mark, primary action and selected options.
- The relevant comparison unit is a fee event with its scope and conditions. Keep issuance distinct from renewal. Separate counter reloads from app reloads. Preserve amounts, periods, currency, limits and unknowns.
- Read customer discussions to discover questions. Use official bank publications for amounts. A captured document does not establish that it is the latest schedule. The [tariff intake notes](tariff-materials.md) and archival index record coverage and gaps.
- Keep bank lists and additional context on demand. Do not use rankings to fill missing evidence.

The dollar-card guide groups reviewed readings by issuance, reload, payment and renewal in a folded comparison. The fee event stays attached to its application scope, native currency and any minimum. Research priorities came from reports of branch-only loading, contradictory renewal prices and unexpected payment fees. Treat these threads as question discovery. Carry the same structure into savings and ATM research by separating an opening deposit from an ongoing minimum balance, and an own-bank ATM withdrawal from another bank's ATM withdrawal. Credit-card research should separate annual fees, late-payment fees and interest. Fixed-deposit comparisons need the published term and withdrawal rule before interpreting the rate. Comparison links can preselect a bank and product. Coverage lists the dated breakdowns separately from official tariff entry points. The comparison worksheet offers the selected banks’ official sources even when a comparable amount is unavailable.

Savings-account notices and account-term pages have their own bounded source monitor. It fingerprints the distinct rate and minimum-balance sources but cannot alter what the reader sees. A changed document is a manual evidence-review trigger; it is not proof that a listed rate, zero minimum or account condition remains current.

The savings journey does not open with a table of institutions. A reader can name a bank they already use, or enter a planned balance and choose one explicit minimum-balance group. The page then reveals only that group; it does not turn a balance into a best-bank ranking.

Savings extraction uses narrow, bank-specific adapters. Everest General Savings, NMB Go-Green and Laxmi Sunrise Savings have strict tested wording for a minimum balance. A parser mismatch produces an unconfirmed candidate, not a guessed value or a new public term.

Keep a required minimum balance separate from the charge for falling below it. Nepal Bank’s Normal Saving row now carries both: NPR 500 required balance and a separately sourced NPR 0 insufficient-minimum-balance fee. Do not infer either fact from the other.
